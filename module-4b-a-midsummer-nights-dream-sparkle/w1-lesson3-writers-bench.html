✏️ Daily Oral Language
✅ Model
Egeus spoke first, but Lysander answered him.

Names of particular people start with a capital letter: Theseus, Hippolyta. When but joins two complete thoughts, a comma comes before it.

🔧 Fix this one
quince gave flute the part of thisbe but flute had a beard coming
Rewrite it correctly.
🔍 Check my work

Quince gave Flute the part of Thisbe, but Flute had a beard coming. Three names take capital letters: Quince, Flute and Thisbe. Because but joins two complete thoughts, a comma comes before it, and the sentence ends with a period.

📖 Read like a Reader

Lysander has just heard the Duke’s ruling. Why does he say this now, and what does it tell Hermia?

✏️ Read like a Writer

Shakespeare writes never did run, an old way to say never ran. Past tense with never sounds like a rule that every story has proved. What does that do to you as a reader?

7th Grade Scholar · the last slice of cake

Version 1: The Complaint

Mom, Dev stole the last slice of my birthday cake. I was saving it for after dinner, and everybody in this house knew that. When I came downstairs, the plate on the counter was empty except for crumbs. I looked everywhere for it. Then he walks right past me with pink frosting on his sleeve. He did not even say sorry. He just sneaked off to his room like nothing happened.

Version 2: From the Inside

I got home from soccer and I was starving. There was one slice of cake on the counter with no note and no name on it. Nobody told me it was hers. I ate it standing up because I had to shower before team pictures. I left the sugar rose on the plate because Priya always likes those best. I even washed my fork in the sink. When she came down, I was already upstairs getting ready for pictures.
