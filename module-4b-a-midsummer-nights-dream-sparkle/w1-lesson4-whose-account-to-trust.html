Big Question
Today’s Wonder

Act 1 is full of people telling stories about someone else, each to a listener, each wanting something. Whose account can we trust, and how would we know?

🎯
Objectives
By the end of this lesson…
📖Reading: I will tell a subjective account from an objective one.
⚖️Argument: I will weigh six Act 1 accounts by who is listening and what the teller wants, then argue whose account we can trust, with two pieces of evidence.
✏️Grammar: I will keep each telling in one time and use had only for what came first.
📝Writing: I will revise a two-version Quick Write for detail, tense and slant.
