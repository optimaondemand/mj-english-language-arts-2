Here is what to hold onto. In this chapter, one thing leads to the next, faster than anyone can stop it. A cause brings an effect, and that effect becomes the next cause. That is cause and effect. Some causes are accidents, and some are choices. Hinton makes you feel how one night can carry two boys a long way from home.
