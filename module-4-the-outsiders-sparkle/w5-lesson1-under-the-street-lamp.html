🔍 Check my work

Two-Bit told Ponyboy that Darry wouldn’t be happy if he fought sick. Two-Bit, Ponyboy and Darry are names, so each takes a capital, and the sentence starts with a capital. Wouldn’t needs its apostrophe (would not is also fine). The words are told, not quoted, so there are no quotation marks. End with a period.

    Part 2Quick Write 5: Claim or CounterclaimStep 1 of 3: plan
    1 Plan2 Draft3 Revise
    Claim or Counterclaim. Option A, a claim: name the moment in Chapter 9 or 10 that you believe is the true climax of the novel, and explain why, with at least one reason tied to what changes in the story because of that moment. Option B, a counterclaim: take a claim of your own about the climax or about a theme in the novel. State the strongest view that disagrees with it, explain why someone could reasonably believe that view, and explain why your claim is still stronger. Both: one paragraph, 150 to 200 words.
