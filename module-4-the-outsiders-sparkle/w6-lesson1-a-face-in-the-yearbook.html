🔍 Check my work

Ponyboy was sick for days after the rumble; Darry and Soda stayed with him. The names Ponyboy, Darry and Soda take capitals. The sentence holds two complete thoughts, and a space alone cannot join them. A semicolon works. So does a period with a capital Darry, or a comma with the word and.

Here is what to hold onto. Chapter Eleven is falling action. The rumble is over, but what it caused is not. Ponyboy is still sick, a hearing is coming, and he will not let himself believe what happened to Johnny. The steps down a story can be as hard as the climb. Watch how a writer uses them to show what the climax cost.

        1 Plan2 Draft3 Revise
        Plan an essay. Choose one theme the book keeps returning to: loyalty, family, or staying gold. Write a working claim: one sentence about what The Outsiders says about that theme. Plan three body points; for each, give a reason and the chapter and event that show it, in your own words. The third may be a complication: something that makes your claim harder, and your answer to it. End with the bigger idea your conclusion will reach.
