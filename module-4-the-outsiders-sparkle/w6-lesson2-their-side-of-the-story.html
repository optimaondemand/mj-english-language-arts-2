🔤 Words: Chapter 12

Say the meaning first, then tap the card to check.

flinching
tap to flip and hear it
flinching
Pulling back or looking away out of fear. · · · “looking straight at the judge, not flinching” · · · (Hinton, Ch. 12)
acquitted
tap to flip and hear it
acquitted
Found not guilty by a court and let go. “Then he said I was acquitted and the whole case was closed.” (Hinton, Ch. 12)
roundabout
tap to flip and hear it
roundabout
Not direct; going the long way around. “At least that was a roundabout way of putting it.” (Hinton, Ch. 12) His teacher never says the word trouble.
vacuum
tap to flip and hear it
vacuum
An empty space; here, a life closed off from everyone. “You're living in a vacuum, Pony, and you're going to have to cut it out.” (Hinton, Ch. 12) Darry means Ponyboy has shut himself off.
vast
tap to flip and hear it
vast
Very great in size or amount. “It was too vast a problem to be just a personal thing.” (Hinton, Ch. 12)
