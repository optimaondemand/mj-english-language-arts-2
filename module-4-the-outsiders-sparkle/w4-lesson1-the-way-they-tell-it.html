🔍 Check my work

Darry carried Ponyboy into the house; he had fallen asleep in the car. The names Darry and Ponyboy take capitals. The sentence holds two complete thoughts, and a comma alone cannot join them. A semicolon works, and so does a period with a capital He. Every sentence needs its end mark.

Here is what to hold onto. Nobody in Chapter Seven stands up and says that loyalty matters. Hinton shows it instead. Darry speaks up for his brother. Soda keeps the reporters laughing in the waiting room. Ponyboy talks to a Soc as one person to another. When choices like these keep pointing at one idea, that idea is an implied theme. You find it by watching, then you name it yourself.
