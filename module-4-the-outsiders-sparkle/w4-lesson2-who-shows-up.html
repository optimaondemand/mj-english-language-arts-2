numbly

ornery
Bad-tempered and hard to get along with. “Dally was his usual mean, ornery self.” (Hinton, Ch. 8)Ponyboy is glad: mean and ornery means Dally is himself again.

helplessness
The feeling that you can do nothing to help or change what happens. “It was the same kind of helplessness” · · · (Hinton, Ch. 8)

charity
Help or money given to people in need. “I wasn’t trying to give you charity, Ponyboy.” (Hinton, Ch. 8)
