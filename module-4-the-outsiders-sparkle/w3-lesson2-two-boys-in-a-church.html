Big Question
Today’s Wonder

Five days in a church can change a person. When someone changes, are they becoming someone new, or showing who they were all along?

Here is what to hold onto. Johnny and Ponyboy both change in these two chapters, and so does the way Ponyboy sees the people around him. That is character development. Hinton shows it through what a character does, what he says, and, for Ponyboy, what he thinks. A dynamic character changes, and a careful reader can point to the moment it happens.
