TODAY'S FOCUS
~12 min
This lesson brings together all eight parts of speech you have studied so far. When you can name every word in a sentence, you can write with more control and read more carefully. Today you will classify a new sentence, asking the questions that identify each part of speech.
THE EIGHT PARTS OF SPEECH
👤
Noun
Names a person, place, thing, or idea
🔄
Pronoun
Replaces a noun (I, he, they, we)
⚡
Verb
Tells what is being said (action, linking, helping)
🌟
Adjective
Describes a noun (What kind? Which one? How many?) Includes article adjective (a/an/the)
🕐
Adverb
Describes a verb, adjective, or adverb (How? When? Where? To what extent?)
📍
Preposition
Shows a relationship; its noun = object of the preposition
🔗
Conjunction
Joins words or groups of words
🤭
Interjection
An exclamation standing apart from the rest of the sentence

👁 READ & NOTICE
HOW TO CLASSIFY THIS SENTENCE
FOCUS SENTENCE
"The tired dog slept quietly under the old wooden porch."

Step 1 What is being said about the dog? The dog slept. Slept is the verb (action verb).

Step 2 Who or what slept? The dog. Dog is the subject noun.

Step 3 The and tired describe dog. The answers "which one": article adjective. Tired answers "what kind": adjective.

Step 4 Slept how? Quietly. Quietly is an adverb (answers "how").

Step 5 Under is a preposition. Porch is the object of the preposition.

Step 6 Old and wooden describe porch; both are adjectives (what kind).

Classified Sentence
The article adj.
tired adjective
dog subject noun
slept verb (action)
quietly adverb
under preposition
the article adj.
old adjective
wooden adjective
porch obj. of prep.
