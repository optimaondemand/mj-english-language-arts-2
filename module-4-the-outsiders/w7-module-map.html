🎯 THE PROJECT YOU ARE BUILDING
The Outsiders Final Project: Loyalty, Family, and Staying Gold
You will assemble two parts. A literary-analysis essay on a theme from the novel (loyalty, family, or staying gold), built from Quick Writes 3 through 6. And a creative component (a character collage or a short social-issue research piece) plus a policy-compliant VR thematic scene and a reflection. The project is the one piece of work this module that your teacher reads closely and grades in depth.

📤 WHAT IS DUE THIS WEEK
Both parts of the Module 4 Final Project are due by the end of Week 7. The two deliverable pages live in Canvas in the Module Wrappers and Project area. Open each one and follow its directions:
The Outsiders Final Project - Essay (assignment): your literary-analysis essay on a theme from the novel (loyalty, family, or staying gold), built from Quick Writes 3 through 6. Submitted as a Word document.
The Outsiders Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment): your creative component (character collage or short social-issue research piece), your VR thematic scene as a video or screenshot, and your 200 to 250 word reflection.
Both components are scored with the same rubric. Read it before you submit so you know what earns full credit.
📁 PULL THESE TOGETHER BEFORE YOU START
You built every piece you need across Weeks 3 through 6. Open your OneDrive and have these ready: Quick Write 3 (theme and evidence paragraph), Quick Write 4 (evidence and explanation), Quick Write 5 (counterclaim and climax), Quick Write 6 (essay outline and intro), and your Reading Journal with any notes on loyalty, family, or staying gold. The two work-session lessons this week walk you through using them.
