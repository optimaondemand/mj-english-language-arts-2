Lesson 6.03 · Weekly Workshop
Putting the Story Together: What Have You Learned?
Lesson ≈10 min
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You have finished the novel. You have built your outline. Today you put it all together and write.
This short lesson is a bridge to Quick Write 6. It gives you a few minutes to look back at everything you have done this module, pull together your best ideas, and move into your Quick Write with momentum. Quick Write 6 is the most important feeder this module: a complete essay outline plus a draft introduction. The final project opens next week.
⚡ BEFORE YOU WRITE: A QUICK LOOK BACK
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
You have read all twelve chapters and worked through six weeks of lessons. Right now, which part of your essay feels most solid? (No wrong answer.)
📚 LOOKING BACK: WHAT THIS MODULE BUILT

Over six weeks you have covered a lot of ground. Here is what your Quick Write 6 can draw from:

Quick Write What you built
QW3A theme + one evidence paragraph (your first claim about loyalty or staying gold)
QW4A second piece of evidence + explanation (adds a body point)
QW5A counterclaim + what truly matters (your claim about the whole novel's climax)
VR 1A theme statement about staying gold in your own words
VR 2Your full outline map: claim, three body points with chapter references, conclusion idea

Quick Write 6 combines all of this into one document: the outline (using the structure from Lesson 6.02) plus a draft introduction. You are not starting from zero. You are assembling what you already built.

➡️ WHAT YOU'LL DO NEXT
1
Quick Write 6
Open the Quick Write 6 assignment page. Write your essay outline plus a draft introduction in Microsoft Word and submit on OneDrive.
2
Check-up
After Quick Write 6, complete the Check-up quiz in Canvas. Then confirm all your submissions before you close out the week.
