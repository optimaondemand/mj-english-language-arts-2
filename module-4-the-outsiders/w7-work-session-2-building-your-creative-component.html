Work Session 2 · Project Week
Building Your Creative Component
Guided work session (ungraded)
~15 min
📖 WHAT THIS SESSION IS FOR
Show the theme, not just say it.
You will plan the creative half of your project. First, choose your creative option: a character collage or a short social-issue research piece. Then plan a VR thematic scene that connects to your chosen theme. Then write a short reflection that ties the whole thing together. By the end you will know exactly what to make for the The Outsiders Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment) page. This session is practice. It is not graded.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED OPEN
▶
Your own copy of The Outsiders and your Reading Journal, open to any notes on loyalty, family, or staying gold.
▶
Your VR headset and your choice of tools for the creative piece: a drawing or design app for a digital collage, or a Word or Pages doc if you are doing the research piece.
▶
The The Outsiders Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment) page (open it in Canvas in the Module Wrappers and Project area).
💡 YOUR TWO CREATIVE OPTIONS
Pick one. Both ask you to do something with the theme, not just describe the plot. Choose whichever fits how you think and work best.
OPTION A · CHARACTER COLLAGE
A visual built around your theme
Create a collage (digital or handmade) that represents your chosen theme through images, symbols, words, and colors connected to the novel. The collage should show the theme, not just name the characters.
Format: digital image, scan, or photo. Submitted with the reflection.
OPTION B · SHORT SOCIAL-ISSUE RESEARCH PIECE
A short research write-up on the novel’s real-world issue
Research one social issue the novel raises (class division, teen conflict, or belonging) using two credible sources. Write a short piece (200 to 300 words) explaining what the issue is and how the novel connects to it. Cite your sources in MLA format.
Format: Word document on OneDrive. Submitted with the reflection.
🧠 PLAN YOUR VR THEMATIC SCENE

Your VR scene is a thematic space: it should make a viewer feel the emotion or tension behind your chosen theme, not recreate a specific scene from the novel. Put on your VR headset and choose any setting you like with enough open ground to arrange objects and labels.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: WHAT YOUR VR SCENE MUST AND MUST NOT DO
Must do: use symbolic or thematic objects, labels, and your own original narration. Choose a setting of your own. The scene expresses a theme concept, not a plot event.

Must not do: recreate a specific scene from the novel, name or portray specific characters, recreate costumes or dialogue from the book, or reproduce any protected text on a label or board. Use original labels and questions only.
1
Choose your setting freely. Open ground, a rooftop, a park, or any other virtual environment works. Your setting does not need to match anything in the novel.
2
Arrange objects or shapes that represent your theme. For loyalty: two objects close together and a barrier between them and the rest. For staying gold: something fragile or bright surrounded by darker shapes. For family: a cluster of different-sized objects facing the same direction. Use what your setting offers.
3
Add two labels or sticky notes in your own words: one that names what the arrangement shows about the theme, and one that asks a question you think the novel raises about it. Both must be your own sentences.
4
When the scene is ready, record a short video or take a screenshot that shows the full arrangement and both labels. If you cannot capture inside VR, write a short description (four to six sentences) of what your scene contains and what it shows about the theme instead.
📚 PLAN YOUR REFLECTION

Your reflection is 200 to 250 words. It connects your creative piece and your VR scene to the theme you analyzed in the essay. It should explain what your choices show about the theme, not just describe what you made.

⚡ TRY IT · IS THIS REFLECTION SENTENCE STRONG OR WEAK?

For each sentence, decide: does it explain an idea about the theme (strong), only describe what was made (weak), or land somewhere in between? Decide first, then choose.

1. “I used gold and black paper and cut out pictures of hands holding together for my collage.”
2. “I used gold to represent what Ponyboy is trying to hold onto, and I let the edges fade to gray to show how the novel says that kind of innocence does not last forever.”
3. “In my VR scene I placed three different-sized objects in a cluster facing the same direction, which felt like the greasers as a family.”
4. “My research showed that class division still separates teenagers in many American cities, which is exactly the barrier the novel argues keeps the greasers and Socs from seeing each other clearly.”
➡️ WHAT YOU’LL DO NEXT
1
Build the three pieces
Complete your creative piece (collage or research write-up), stage and capture your VR thematic scene, and write your 200 to 250 word reflection. Submit them on the The Outsiders Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment) page.
2
Run the Submission Checklist
Before you turn anything in, open the W7 - Submission Checklist page and confirm every piece of both components is ready.
💪 BEFORE YOU GO

You read the whole novel, built your thinking across six Quick Writes, and now you are turning that thinking into something you made. Choose an option that lets you show what the theme means to you. Build the VR scene around that same idea. Then let your reflection tie all three pieces together so a reader can see what you understood.
