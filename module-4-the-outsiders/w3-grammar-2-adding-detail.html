Grammar 2
Adding Detail: Expanding Sentences with Adjectives, Adverbs, and Prepositional Phrases
Lesson ≈12 min
📝 TODAY'S FOCUS
A bare sentence gives the facts. A detailed sentence makes the reader see something. Today you will learn how to expand a sentence by adding adjectives (describers of nouns), adverbs (describers of verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs), and prepositional phrases. Each addition adds precision and texture.
📚 THIS WEEK'S SENTENCE (original example, Module 4 context)
Bare: “The boy read.”
Expanded: “The restless boy read quietly in the shadowed corner of the old church.”
⚡ WARM-UP
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
Read “The boy read” and then “The restless boy read quietly in the shadowed corner of the old church.” What does the second sentence do that the first does not? (No wrong answer.)
💡 THREE WAYS TO EXPAND A SENTENCE
Adjective
Describes a noun. Ask: What kind? Which one? How many?

“The restless boy read.”
What kind of boy? Restless.
Adverb
Describes a verb, adjective, or adverb. Ask: How? When? Where? To what extent?

“The boy read quietly.”
How did the boy read? Quietly.
Prepositional Phrase
A preposition plus its object of the preposition. Adds location, time, or direction.

“The boy read in the corner.”
Where? In the corner. Prep: in. Object: corner.
📚 LABELING THE EXPANDED SENTENCE

Here is the full expanded sentence: “The restless boy read quietly in the shadowed corner of the old church.” Walk through it using the questions:

  1. Who or what read? The boy. The word boy is the subject noun.
  2. What is being said about the boy? He read. That is the verb (action verb).
  3. The: what kind of adjective is “the”? An article adjective.
  4. What kind of boy? Restless. That is an adjective describing the subject noun.
  5. How did the boy read? Quietly. That is an adverb describing the verb.
  6. Where did he read? In the shadowed corner of the old church. That is a chain of prepositional phrases. The prepositions are in and of; their objects of the preposition are corner and church. The adjectives shadowed and old describe those nouns.

✏️ APPLY IT IN YOUR WRITING

Start with this bare sentence: “The sky changed.”

In your Reading Journal, expand it three times: once by adding at least one adjective, once by adding at least one adverb, and once by adding at least one prepositional phrase. Then write one final sentence that uses all three. Label each addition in brackets afterward.
