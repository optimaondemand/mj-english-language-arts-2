Thesis Statement
Lesson (ungraded)
~50 min
📖 WHAT THIS LESSON IS FOR
An argument starts with one sentence that tells the reader exactly what you will prove.
You have been thinking about heroism, disguise, and courage across this whole module. Today you take that thinking and sharpen it into a thesis: one specific, arguable sentence that answers the question "What kind of courage does the novel celebrate, and is the Pimpernel's disguise heroic or deceptive?" By the end you will draft your own thesis and add it to your outline document in your Reading Journal.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED OPEN
▶
Your Quick Write 3 (your thesis about loyalty and heroism) from OneDrive.
▶
A new Word document saved in your OneDrive course folder, named LastName_SP_EssayOutline_ELA7. This is where you will build the outline all week.
💡 THE SHAPE OF A STRONG THESIS
A thesis for this essay must do three things. Every one of them matters.
PART A · TAKE A POSITION
Decide: is the Pimpernel's disguise heroic, or deceptive, or something more complicated than either? Your thesis picks a side and names it.
PART B · NAME THE KIND OF COURAGE
The question asks what kind of courage the novel celebrates. Name it: is it physical bravery, moral courage, the courage to sacrifice reputation, or something else? A strong thesis does not just say "courage" without saying which kind.
PART C · GIVE A REASON, NOT JUST AN OPINION
The word "because" is your friend. A thesis that says "the disguise is heroic because it requires sacrificing how others see you in order to save lives" gives the reader something to follow. A thesis that just says "the disguise is heroic" leaves nothing to prove.
📚 READ AND NOTICE: A WORKED EXAMPLE

Here is a complete thesis for this essay. Notice how all three parts are present. This is a model for the moves, not an answer to copy. You will argue for your own position with your own wording.

POSITION The Pimpernel's disguise is heroic rather than deceptive KIND OF COURAGE because it requires a specific kind of moral courage: the willingness to be seen as foolish and worthless so that others can live. REASON Orczy shows that the hardest part of the Pimpernel's mission is not crossing enemy lines but enduring Marguerite's contempt while keeping the secret that would explain everything.

Notice that the thesis commits to a view, names a specific kind of courage (moral, not physical), and gives a reason grounded in the novel. You do not need to agree with this position. Pick the one you can argue for best.

⚡ CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Read each sentence. Decide whether it is a strong thesis, a weak thesis (too vague or no reason), or not a thesis at all. Decide first, then choose.

1. “The Pimpernel’s disguise is heroic because he is brave.”
2. “The novel celebrates the courage to appear cowardly, because the Pimpernel can only save lives by letting his wife and society believe he is a fool.”
3. “Sir Percy Blakeney uses a disguise to rescue people from the guillotine.”
4. “The Pimpernel’s disguise is ultimately deceptive because it depends on deceiving the one person whose trust he values most: his own wife.”
🧠 NOW DRAFT YOUR THESIS

Open your Quick Write 3. Look at the thesis line you wrote there. In your Reading Journal outline document, write one thesis sentence that does all three things: takes a position on whether the disguise is heroic or deceptive, names the kind of courage the novel celebrates, and gives a reason grounded in the novel.

Label it Thesis at the top of your outline document. Save it to OneDrive.
You will revise it after you find your evidence. A thesis often gets sharper once you see which details best support it.
