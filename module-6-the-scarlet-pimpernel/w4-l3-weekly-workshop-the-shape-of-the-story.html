📚 PART 2 · CONNECTING TO YOUR ARGUMENT

Two kinds of courage in six chapters

Your Module 6 argument asks what kind of courage the novel celebrates and whether the Pimpernel's disguise is heroic or something more complicated. Ch. 11–16 gives you two kinds of evidence. The first is what the Pimpernel does: he does not appear at the supper-room when the trap is set, which means his network of deception is still holding. His absence is itself a kind of action. The second is what Marguerite does: she trades her integrity for Armand's safety and ends the section more isolated than she began. Both kinds of courage involve risk and cost. For your Quick Write today, you will write one evidence paragraph using a specific moment from Ch. 11–16 to support your claim.
