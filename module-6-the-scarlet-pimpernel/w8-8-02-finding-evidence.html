📖 WHAT THIS LESSON IS FOR
Evidence on its own proves nothing. The explanation is what does the work.
Today you pick the two or three strongest moments from the novel that support your thesis, and you write an explanation for each one. The explanation is the sentence that says what the detail shows about courage and disguise. Without that sentence, evidence is just plot summary.

▶
Your Quick Write 4 (evidence paragraph) from OneDrive.
▶

💡 THE EVIDENCE + EXPLANATION FORMULA
Each evidence point in your outline needs two pieces, side by side. Here is what each one does.
THE EVIDENCE
A specific moment from the novel
Either in your own words (a paraphrase) or a short direct quote (four to eight words, in quotation marks). Never just name a chapter. Point to something that actually happens or that a character actually says.
THE EXPLANATION
What the moment shows about your thesis
This is your voice. It begins by connecting the detail to your argument: "This shows that..." or "Orczy uses this moment to demonstrate..." It never retells the plot. It reaches the idea.
How to cite: In the outline, note the author’s last name and the chapter in parentheses after the evidence. For a paraphrase: (Orczy, Ch. 14). For a direct quote: ("exact words here," Orczy, Ch. 14). Keep quotes short. Your explanation should be longer than your quote. If you already name Orczy in your own sentence, do not name her again in the parentheses; just give the chapter.
📚 READ AND NOTICE: A WORKED EXAMPLE

Here is one complete evidence point, written exactly as it would appear in an outline. Notice the two parts and how they work together. This example argues that the disguise is heroic.

EVIDENCE When Marguerite challenges her husband, Percy Blakeney responds with exaggerated boredom and empty talk of fashion, giving her no reason to suspect he is the Scarlet Pimpernel (Orczy, Ch. 10).
EXPLANATION This shows the particular kind of courage the novel values: the willingness to let the person you love think the worst of you, because revealing the truth would endanger her and the men he is rescuing. The heroism is in the sacrifice, not the swashbuckling.

Notice that the explanation does not just say "this is an example of heroism." It names exactly what kind of heroism and why. Your explanation should do the same for your own evidence.

⚡ CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Below are sentences that follow a piece of evidence. For each one, decide: does it explain what the detail shows about courage and disguise, or does it only retell the plot?

1. “This shows that Percy acts like he does not care about serious things and only thinks about fashion.”
2. “This demonstrates that the kind of courage Orczy values is not physical but relational: Percy risks losing Marguerite’s respect rather than risk her life.”
3. “Orczy uses this scene to show that the disguise is not costless: the Pimpernel's greatest risk is not the guillotine but the loss of genuine closeness with his wife.”
4. “Chauvelin is the villain who tries to catch the Scarlet Pimpernel and uses Marguerite as a way to get to him.”
🧠 NOW ADD YOUR EVIDENCE TO THE OUTLINE

Open your Quick Write 4 and your outline document. Below your thesis, add two or three evidence points. Each one needs the evidence (a specific moment, in your own words or a short quote with the author’s last name and a chapter number) and the explanation (what it shows about courage and disguise). Use your Quick Write 4 as your starting material, and add a second detail of your own from anywhere in the novel.

After you add the evidence, re-read your thesis from 8.01. Does it still match what your evidence is proving? If not, sharpen the thesis now. It is normal to adjust it at this stage.
Save the updated outline to OneDrive when you are done.
📜 YOUR WORKS CITED ENTRY
You are citing in MLA style, the style used for writing about literature and the one every essay in this course uses. Your finished essay ends with one Works Cited entry for the novel:
Orczy, Baroness. The Scarlet Pimpernel. 1905.
