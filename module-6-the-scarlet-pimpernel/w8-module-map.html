What this week includes
❓ THE ARGUMENT QUESTION YOU ARE ANSWERING
“What kind of courage does the novel celebrate, and is the Pimpernel’s disguise heroic or deceptive?”
You have been building toward this question all module. Your Quick Writes hold your best thinking. This week you organize that thinking into a five-part outline: thesis, two or three evidence points with explanation, a counterargument and response, and a conclusion. Next week you write the full essay from this outline.
