🎯 THE PROJECT YOU ARE BUILDING
Heroism in Disguise: What Kind of Courage Does The Scarlet Pimpernel Celebrate?
You will complete two parts. A written argumentative essay answering the module question, built from your Quick Writes 3 through 7 and the Week 8 outline. And a creative component and VR thematic scene (student choice) staging the novel’s themes of heroism and disguise, plus a short reflection. The project is the one piece of work this module that your teacher reads closely and grades in depth.

📤 WHAT IS DUE THIS WEEK
Both parts of the Module 6 Final Project are due by the end of Week 9. The two deliverable pages live in Canvas in the Module Wrappers and Project area. Open each one and follow its directions:
Scarlet Pimpernel Final Project - Essay (assignment): your argumentative essay on the kind of courage the novel celebrates. Built from Quick Writes 3 through 7 and your Week 8 outline. Submitted as a Word document.
Scarlet Pimpernel Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment): your creative component (student choice), your VR thematic scene staging heroism or disguise as a video or screenshot, and your 200 to 250 word reflection.
Both components are scored with the same rubric on the project page. Read it before you submit so you know what earns full credit.
