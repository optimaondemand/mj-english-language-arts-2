📋 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
The Module 6 Final Project has two components, submitted on two separate pages. Check off each item below as it is confirmed. When every box is checked, the project is ready to submit.
✏️ COMPONENT 1 · ARGUMENTATIVE ESSAY
Submitted as a Word document on the Scarlet Pimpernel Final Project - Essay (assignment) page.
☐
My thesis states clearly what kind of courage the novel celebrates and takes a position on whether the Pimpernel’s disguise is heroic or deceptive.
☐
I give two pieces of evidence from the novel, each one a specific, pointable detail from the text.
☐
After each piece of evidence I wrote an explanation that says what it shows about courage or disguise in my own words, not just a retelling of the plot.
☐
My essay includes a counterargument that names the opposing view specifically, gives it a fair hearing, and explains why my claim holds anyway.
☐
My conclusion restates the point in fresh words and says what it means about heroism or courage beyond the novel’s plot.
☐
The doc is saved to OneDrive and submitted on the Scarlet Pimpernel Final Project - Essay (assignment) page.
🎨 COMPONENT 2 · CREATIVE COMPONENT
Submitted on the Scarlet Pimpernel Final Project - Creative (assignment) page.
☐
My creative component (illustrated scene, symbol arrangement, comic strip, or character profile) is finished and makes my chosen theme of heroism or disguise visible to a viewer without just illustrating a plot summary.
☐
My reflection is 200 to 250 words and explains what my creative component shows about heroism or disguise. It names at least one specific choice I made and connects my work to Orczy’s argument in the novel.
☐
Both pieces are uploaded and submitted on the Scarlet Pimpernel Final Project - Creative (assignment) page.
✅ TWO LAST CHECKS
☐
I read the rubric on the project pages and checked my work against all of its criteria, including intellectual honesty: every idea in my essay is my own or is clearly grounded in the novel.
☐
Both components are submitted by the end of Week 9.
