📌 Watch how Priya planned her cover
Priya’s cover plan
Priya · 7th Grade Scholar

Symbol: a candle. Palette: Midnight Bells. Lettering: Copperplate, title at the top. Back cover: Three visits, one night, and a man who has to decide.

Why this symbol? A candle is small, but it lights a whole dark room, the way each visit lights up one thing Scrooge has been missing.

Why these colors? Dark green is the cold night. Gold is the warm light he lets in at the end.

What should a reader expect? A ghost story that is also about being warm to other people.

Notice: every choice has a reason, and each reason points back to the book.
