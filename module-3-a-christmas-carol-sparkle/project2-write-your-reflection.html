📌 Watch how Priya wrote her answer
Priya’s answer
Priya · 7th Grade Scholar

Memory starts the change in Scrooge, but love finishes it. When Scrooge asks, “tell me if Tiny Tim will live” (Dickens, Stave III), he is worrying about a child who is not his, and nobody is scaring him into it. That is love, not fear.

Notice: claim first, then the moment, then what it shows. Her quotation is short and carries its citation.
