✅ Model

The owner is one person, so the apostrophe comes before the s. Two complete thoughts joined by and take a comma before the and.

Rewrite it correctly.
🔍 Check my work

Peter wore his father’s shirt collar, and the two young Cratchits danced about the table. Names take capital letters. Father’s has one owner, so the apostrophe comes before the s. Cratchits is a plain plural with nothing to own, so it takes no apostrophe. A comma comes before and when it joins two complete thoughts.

Priya
7th Grade Scholar · her draft

I think the Ghost of Christmas Present does the most to change Scrooge. The Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come scares him, but fear only lasts while you are scared. The second Spirit makes him care about real people. At Fred’s party Scrooge plays the guessing games even though nobody can hear him, and he “begged like a boy to be allowed to stay” (Dickens, Stave III). On Christmas Eve he would not even come to dinner. At the Cratchits house he asks if Tiny Tim will live “with an interest he had never felt before” (Dickens, Stave III). Someone could say the grave is what really changes him, because he falls on his knees and begs. But I think he was already changing before the grave, and the grave just made him hurry. The Spirit shows him Bob’s family, Fred’s games, and how the miners were singing. A person can change when they stop only looking at themselves.
