This lesson brings together all eight parts of speech you have studied so far. When you can name every word in a sentence, you can write with more control and read more carefully. Today you will classify a new sentence, asking the questions that identify each part of speech.
THE EIGHT PARTS OF SPEECH
👤
Noun
Names a person, place, thing, or idea
🔄
Pronoun
Replaces a noun (I, he, they, we)
⚡
Verb
Tells what is being said (action, linking, helping)
🌟
Adjective
Describes a noun (What kind? Which one? How many?). Includes article adjective (a/an/the)
🕐
Adverb
Describes a verb, adjective, or adverb (How? When? Where? To what extent?)
📍
Preposition
Shows a relationship; its noun = object of the preposition
🔗
Conjunction
Joins words or groups of words
🤭
Interjection
An exclamation standing apart from the rest of the sentence

👁 READ & NOTICE
HOW TO CLASSIFY THIS SENTENCE
FOCUS SENTENCE
“The mischievous fairy quietly dropped shimmering juice on the sleeping traveler.”

Step 1 What is being said about the fairy? The fairy dropped juice. Dropped is the verb (action verb).

Step 2 Who or what dropped? The fairy. Fairy is the subject noun.

Step 3 The and mischievous describe fairy. The answers “which one”: article adjective. Mischievous answers “what kind”: adjective.

Step 4 Dropped how? Quietly. Quietly is an adverb (answers “how”).

Step 5 Shimmering describes juice (what kind: adjective). Juice receives the action: noun (direct object).

Step 6 On is a preposition. Sleeping describes traveler (what kind: adjective). Traveler is the object of the preposition.
