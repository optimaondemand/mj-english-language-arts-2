📋 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE
Your Module 4B Final Project has two components, and they are submitted on two separate pages in the Module Wrappers and Project area. Work down each list below and check off each item as you confirm it. When every box is checked, you are ready to submit.
✏️ COMPONENT 1 · LITERARY ANALYSIS ESSAY
Submitted as a Word document on the MND Final Project - Essay (assignment) page.
☐
My thesis names whose account of the night in the wood I find most believable (or explains why no single account is fully trustworthy) and states what the play suggests about why people tell the stories they tell.
☐
I give two or three pieces of evidence from the play, each cited in MLA format (Shakespeare’s name plus act.scene).
☐
My essay ends with one Works Cited entry for the play.
☐
After each piece of evidence I wrote an explanation that says what it shows about whose story to believe, in my own words.
☐
My conclusion restates the point in fresh words and says what the play suggests about why people tell the stories they tell.
☐
The doc is saved on OneDrive and submitted on the MND Final Project - Essay (assignment) page.
🎨 COMPONENT 2 · CREATIVE PIECE, VR SCENE, AND REFLECTION
Submitted on the MND Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment) page.
☐
My creative piece (staging design, character map of who believes what, or illustrated scene) connects clearly to the module question and goes beyond a simple retelling of events.
☐
My VR scene stages one moment where a character tells a story others must judge, uses my own original labels, and does not depend on any single named VR platform. I chose my own setting and captured it as a short video or screenshot, or wrote a description if I could not capture inside VR.
☐
My reflection is 200 to 250 words and explains what my creative and VR choices show about the module question, not just what I made.
☐
All three pieces are uploaded and submitted on the MND Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment) page.
✅ TWO LAST CHECKS
☐
I reviewed the rubric for the project and checked my work against all of its criteria, including intellectual honesty: every claim in my essay and reflection is my own interpretation, expressed in my own words, and I named where my case is strong and where it is less certain.
☐
Both components are submitted by the end of Week 7.
🎉
You have finished Module 4B.
You read an entire play, followed six different characters as they told their own stories about one strange night, and turned what you understood into an essay, a creative piece, and a scene you built yourself. That is real work. Submit with confidence, and carry one idea forward from this module: Puck asked the audience to judge the whole play as gently as they would judge a dream. The characters inside the play were asking each other for the very same thing.
