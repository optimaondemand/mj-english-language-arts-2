🎯 THE PROJECT YOU ARE BUILDING
A Midsummer Night’s Dream Final Project: Whose Story Do We Believe?
You will assemble two parts. A literary-analysis essay that answers the module question by naming which account of the night in the wood you find most credible, built from Quick Writes 3 through 6. And a creative component (a staging design, a character map of who believes what, or an illustrated scene) plus a VR scene staging one moment where a character tells a story others must judge, and a reflection. The project is the one piece of work this module that your teacher reads closely and grades in depth.

📤 WHAT IS DUE THIS WEEK
Both parts of the Module 4B Final Project are due by the end of Week 7. The two deliverable pages live in Canvas in the Module Wrappers and Project area. Open each one and follow its directions:
MND Final Project - Essay (assignment): your literary-analysis essay answering whose account of the night in the wood you find most believable, built from Quick Writes 3 through 6. Submitted as a Word document.
MND Final Project - Creative + VR (assignment): your creative component, your VR scene as a video or screenshot, and your 200 to 250 word reflection.
Both components are scored with the same rubric. Read it before you submit so you know what earns full credit.
📁 PULL THESE TOGETHER BEFORE YOU START
You built every piece you need across Weeks 3 through 6. Open your OneDrive and have these ready: Quick Write 3 (evidence paragraph), Quick Write 4 (evidence and explanation), Quick Write 5 (counterclaim), Quick Write 6 (outline and intro), and your Reading Journal with your notes on who narrates what in the play: Egeus to Theseus, Titania on the changeling boy and the seasons, Helena on her friendship with Hermia, Bottom waking from his vision, Theseus and Hippolyta arguing over the lovers’ story, and Puck’s epilogue. The two work-session lessons this week walk you through using them.
