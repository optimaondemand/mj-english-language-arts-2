📝 TODAY’S FOCUS
⌚ ~12 min
Proofreading is not the same skill as writing. Writing gets your ideas down. Proofreading is the last, separate pass where you hunt for exactly the kinds of errors that are easy to miss in your own work. You will use this checklist on your own draft of Quick Write 6.
✅ A FIVE-STEP PROOFREADING CHECKLIST
👀
Read it aloud. Your ear catches mistakes your eye skips past.
🤝
Check subject and verb. Ask “what is being said about the subject” for each sentence, and make sure the verb matches the subject.
🔍
Check confused words. Their/there/they’re, its/it’s, your/you’re, then/than.
📌
Check end punctuation. Every sentence ends with the right mark, and no sentence runs into the next.
📄
Check capitals. Character names, place names, and act and scene numbers are capitalized.
📚 ONE PARAGRAPH, BEFORE AND AFTER

Here is a short paragraph with several errors, and the same paragraph after a proofreading pass. Compare them line by line.

BEFORE
theseus dont believe the lovers story at first he thinks its just imagination. hippolyta think different then him, she says the story is more convincing then he admits.
AFTER
Theseus does not believe the lovers’ story at first. He thinks it’s just imagination. Hippolyta thinks differently than him. She says the story is more convincing than he admits.
⚡ TRY IT · WHICH CHECKLIST STEP CATCHES THIS?
1. “puck speaks the final lines of the play.”
2. “The fairies met their, in the wood outside Athens.”
3. “Theseus doubts the story, Hippolyta finds it convincing.”
4. “The mechanicals performs their play for the whole court.”
✏️ APPLY IT

Before you submit Quick Write 6 later this week, run your own draft through all five checklist steps, in order. Fix anything you find.
