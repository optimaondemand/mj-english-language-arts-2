📅 A NOTE ABOUT THIS WEEK
This is the poetry week. Shakespeare does not write the whole play the same way. Some characters speak in a steady, unrhymed beat. Some speak in rhyming pairs. The workmen just talk. Day 1's lesson teaches you to hear the difference, and every lesson after it points back to that skill.

🎯 THIS WEEK'S QUESTION
Everyone in this play tells a story about what happened in the wood. Whose account can we trust, and how would we know?
This week, Bottom gets an unwanted new look, Titania falls for him under a spell, and Helena tells the story of a friendship she believes has just been betrayed. Notice how each of these moments is a story someone tells about themselves or about someone else.
